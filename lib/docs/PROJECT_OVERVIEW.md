# Dental Clinic Management System - Project Overview

## 📋 Project Information

**Project Name:** Dental Clinic Management System (DCMS)  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Development Methodology:** Scrum/Agile  
**Platform:** Flutter (Cross-platform)  
**Backend:** Supabase (PostgreSQL + Auth + Storage)  
**Start Date:** January 2026  
**Status:** Active Development

---

## 🎯 Vision Statement

To provide dental clinics with a comprehensive, user-friendly management system that streamlines patient care, appointment scheduling, billing, and administrative tasks, enabling healthcare professionals to focus on delivering exceptional dental services.

---

## 🎭 Stakeholders

### Primary Users

- **Administrators** - Full system access and management
- **Doctors/Dentists** - Patient care and medical records
- **Receptionists** - Scheduling and front desk operations

### Secondary Stakeholders

- Patients (indirect beneficiaries)
- Clinic Owners
- IT Support Staff

---

## 🚀 Core Features

### 1. **Authentication & Authorization**

- Secure login (Email/Password + Face ID)
- Role-based access control (Admin, Doctor, Receptionist)
- Password reset functionality
- Session management

### 2. **Dashboard**

- Real-time statistics and KPIs
- Appointment overview
- Revenue analytics
- Quick access widgets

### 3. **Patient Management**

- Complete patient profiles
- Medical history tracking
- Dental records
- Allergies management
- Prescription history
- Profile image upload
- Contact information

### 4. **Appointment Scheduling**

- Calendar-based scheduling
- Appointment status tracking
- Doctor assignment
- Conflict detection
- Notifications

### 5. **Billing & Financial Management**

- Invoice generation
- Payment tracking
- Treatment pricing
- Expense management
- Financial reports

### 6. **Settings & Configuration**

- Clinic information management
- User profile settings
- System preferences
- Multi-language support (French)

---
